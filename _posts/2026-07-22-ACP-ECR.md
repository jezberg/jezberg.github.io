I am very happy to let you know that the [Association of Constraint Programming has awarded me the 2026 Early Career Researcher Award.](https://www.a4cp.org/awards/early-career-research-award) The ACP Early Career Researcher Award is awarded every year to a researcher who has completed their PhD at most 8 years ago, with a scientific track record showing great promise in Constraint Programming. This year the honor fell to me.

I want to thank the ACP and the CP community for the honor, and also the people who nominated me. I look forward ot being an active member of the community for many years to come.
